# Changelog

## [0.2.0] - 2026-09-02

- Expanded the registry from 13 to 21 physical instrument records using a
  review-defined 19-system performance core plus labelled planned/regional additions.
- Added 22 claim-level performance records with metric, target, baseline,
  measurement context, caveat, primary source and access date.
- Corrected KPF, HARPS3 and PARVI current-status framing.
- Added a claim ledger, research gap matrix, manuscript draft and reproducible
  third figure showing why unlike precision metrics cannot form a leaderboard.
- Added a responsive, accessible, filterable research website.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-02

- Curated 13 major optical/NIR EPRV spectrographs with primary references
  and dated status sources.
- Separated design requirements, laboratory calibration, commissioning,
  and on-sky evidence.
- Added a nine-part challenge evidence table, research synthesis, schema
  validation, CLI summaries, tests, and repository-native figures.
