Interpretation

Nine coupled error domains

No component operates alone; improving one term can expose another.

  1. Stellar variabilityOscillations, granulation, spots and faculae reshape spectral lines.
  2. Photon informationThroughput, spectrum and rotation matter with resolution.
  3. Calibration transferReference light does not perfectly share the stellar path.
  4. Fibre illuminationScrambling and modal noise couple seeing and guiding into the spectrum.
  5. Instrument erasMaintenance and thermal cycles create offsets and state changes.
  6. Detector physicsPixel geometry, charge transfer, persistence and non-linearity matter.
  7. TelluricsMasking loses information; modelling introduces atmospheric uncertainty.
  8. Barycentric timingThe effective exposure midpoint can be wavelength dependent.
  9. PipelinesExtraction, templates and outlier policy shape the reported statistic.
