# Limitations

- The 13-instrument sample is curated, not a statistically complete census.
- Nominal wavelength bounds do not represent throughput, detector gaps,
  telluric loss, blaze response, or target-dependent Doppler information.
- A single representative resolving power suppresses multi-mode and
  wavelength-dependent line-spread-function details.
- Published “precision” values are definition-dependent. The repository
  therefore preserves prose and evidence class rather than manufacturing
  a league table.
- On-sky scatter combines the star, atmosphere, photon statistics,
  cadence, telescope, instrument, and reduction pipeline.
- Operational status is time-sensitive. The status date and source are
  required, but a newer observatory notice always supersedes this snapshot.
- The report is a structured literature synthesis, not an independent
  re-reduction of raw spectra from all instruments.
- No paper figures, instrument logos, or copyrighted diagrams are copied.
  Repository figures are generated from the original synthesis table.
- ANDES and HARPS3 entries describe planned/commissioning systems; their
  requirements must not be read as demonstrated performance.
